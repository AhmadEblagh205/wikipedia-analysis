Catalysis increases the rate of a chemical reaction by providing an
alternative pathway with a lower activation energy. The catalyst is not
consumed by the reaction. Heterogeneous catalysis, where the catalyst is in
a different phase from the reactants, dominates industrial chemistry: solid
catalysts convert gases in processes such as ammonia synthesis and methanol
synthesis. Catalyst activity, selectivity and deactivation over time decide
the economics of a catalytic process.
